Patterns of Resemblance Calculator

Syntax

+ sum, * or × product, ω^x (or w^x) exponential, x^3 repeated product, log(x), simp(x), amal(x, y).

Compare with < > <= >= ==. Name a result with G = Γ₀ + 1.

Refer to earlier results by name, by $3 (history entry), Ans (last result), or #id (content hash, any unique prefix). Raw patterns: [P1;0;2;2=1.1].

Built-ins: w/ω/omega, e0/ε₀/epsilon_0, G0/Γ₀/Gamma_0. Any spelled-out Greek name with an optional _n subscript (e.g. delta_1) is the same as the symbol (δ₁). Double-click a history card to insert it.
